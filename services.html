Services

Contract farming, birds at every layer stage, and practical consultancy for growing farms.

Layers

Layer contract farming

AVSL supplies layer day-old chicks to participating farmers and supports rearing up to about 2½ months of age. Thereafter, the company buys back the birds under agreed terms.

Quantities, pricing, quality requirements, timelines and buy-back conditions are agreed before commencement.

Birds beyond day-old

  • Pullets

    Developed young layer birds beyond early brooding.

  • Point-of-lay (POL)

    Birds approaching the start of egg production.

  • Active layers

    Mature, productive stock for established egg enterprises.

Broilers

Broiler contract farming

Participating farmers receive a 40% support contribution on broiler feed costs, subject to contract terms — reducing production burden with a structured market pathway.

Quantities, pricing, feed support, timelines and performance expectations are agreed before start.
