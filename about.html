About AVSL

A poultry-focused company committed to partnering with farmers for better, more profitable farming.

Who we are

Built around farmer success

AVSL Chicks Ventures Ltd supplies day-old broiler and layer chicks, poultry cages, feeds, feeding equipment and accessories. We serve small-scale, medium-scale and commercial farmers across Kenya, with a long-term vision of expanding into East Africa.

We provide quality products, equipment and practical solutions designed to make poultry farming easier, more reliable and more profitable.

Vision

To become a leading and trusted poultry solutions company in Kenya and East Africa — empowering farmers with quality products, reliable services and innovative solutions.

Mission

To provide quality chicks, feeds, cages, feeding equipment and other poultry solutions that help farmers start, operate and grow successful enterprises.

Commitment

Honest communication, product availability, competitive prices, reliable supply and responsive service. When farmers grow, we grow with them.
